## Full description (max 4000)
```
GeoBharat is a quiz and learning app for the geography of India. Pick your level and start playing in seconds.

CHOOSE YOUR LEVEL
Class 6-8, Class 9-10, Class 11-12, College, State PCS and UPSC. The questions get harder as you go up, so the app is right for a school student and for an exam aspirant.

ENDLESS QUESTIONS, NO REPEATS
Play 10-question rounds or switch on endless mode. GeoBharat remembers what you have already answered and does not repeat a question. It keeps making fresh questions from its fact tables, even without internet.

LEARN, NOT ONLY QUIZ
The Learn section explains 14 topics: the Himalayas, Northern Plains, Peninsular Plateau, Thar Desert, coasts and islands, rivers, monsoon, soils, forests, lakes, states and capitals, borders and mountain passes. Each lesson matches your level. A Class 6 lesson is short and simple. A UPSC lesson is long and detailed, with exam traps and Mains-style questions. You can also listen to a lesson read aloud.

DAILY REMINDER
Set a time and your phone reminds you to play, even when the app is closed. Build a daily streak.

HOME-SCREEN WIDGET
Add the GeoBharat widget to your home screen for a new geography fact every day. Tap the arrow for the next fact, or tap the widget to open the quiz.

SCOREBOARD
See your best scores for every level and category, and your recent games.

HINDI AND ENGLISH
Switch language at any time.

WORKS OFFLINE
Questions, lessons and reminders all work without internet. No ads. No account. No tracking.
```
