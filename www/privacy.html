GeoBharat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

GeoBharat is an India geography quiz and learning app. We respect your privacy, and the app is built to work without collecting any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. GeoBharat has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

Your scores, streak, chosen level, language and reminder time are saved only on your device so the app can remember them. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes them. This information is never sent to us.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks your permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your phone and works without an internet connection. You can turn it off in the app or in your phone settings at any time.

Widget

The home-screen widget shows built-in geography facts. It does not read or send any data.

Children

The app contains no ads, no external links and no user-generated content, and it does not ask for personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
